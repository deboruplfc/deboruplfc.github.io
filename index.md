## About Me

Hello there! 👋

I am a CS Ph.D. Student, fortunately advised by [Prof. Bryan S. Kim](https://web.ecs.syr.edu/~bkim01/index.html) at [Syracuse University](https://www.syracuse.edu/). I'm broadly interested in computer systems research, especially in storage systems, memory systems, computer architecture, and operating systems. I completed my M.S. in Computer Science at [University at Buffalo](https://www.buffalo.edu/), where I worked on data transfer optimization for [network behavior forecasting](https://github.com/didclab/onedatashare). I hold a B.Eng. in Computer Engineering from the erstwhile [NSIT](https://nsut.ac.in/en/home), then part of [University of Delhi](https://www.du.ac.in/).

I also have 3+ years of Software & Platform Engineering experience. Following graduation, I joined the Chief Data Office at [Nomura](https://www.nomura.com/) where I built systems handling ~10M trades daily with real-time processing. I was also involved in designing and maintaining Data & ML platforms in both microservice & monolithic architectures. In the past, I was a research assistant at [The University of Texas at Dallas](https://utdallas.edu/), spent two summers as a research intern at [Illinois Institute of Technology](https://www.iit.edu/) and a summer at [Qatar Computing Research Institute](https://www.hbku.edu.qa/en/qcri).
